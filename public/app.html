What the admin view looks like

        

          

            
            
Admin: Schedule tab with tabs, filters, and shift boxes you can tap to edit

          

          

            
            
Admin: Ask Q-Scheduler — plain-language questions, instant answers

          

        

      


      

        
Schedule views

        

          
Week view — Day/Week/Month/Range tabs, Department and Shift filters

          
Settings → Shifts — define your own shift types, colors, and times

          
Settings → Staffing — daily targets per shift type, highlights short-staffed days

        

        
The Schedule tab has three views: Day, Week, and Range.

        
    
          
  • Day view — see everyone scheduled for one date. Tap a shift box to reassign, swap, or remove someone. The ⋮ button between the shift type and name opens shift actions.
    • 
          
  • Week view — seven days at a glance with full shift names.
    • 
          
  • Range view — pick any date range.
    • 
        

        
Filter by Department or Shift using the two dropdowns under the heading. Green dates are published, gray are draft, plain are empty.

        
Publishing: assign shifts as drafts, then publish each shift when it's final. Staff only see published shifts on shared links — the date itself doesn't need to be published.

      


      

        
Ask Q-Scheduler

        

          
Ask Q-Scheduler — plain-language questions, instant answers

        

        
Ask plain-language questions about the schedule, like "Who is on tonight?", "Where are we short next week?", or "How many shifts did Priya work in September?" Answers cover the whole schedule; draft shifts are labeled (draft).

        
Staffing targets: set how many people you need per shift type per day (e.g. 4 Day Hospitalists + 1 Night Hospitalist + 1 Night NP). Ask "where are we short?" to find gaps.

        
Change plans (email-to-schedule)

        
Forward a schedule email to your connected inbox address and the system reads it, builds a proposed change plan, and emails you for approval — you never have to check email yourself.

        
    
          
  • Change requests ("assign Maya to Day Hospitalist on Oct 12") → a plan appears under Change plans and you get an email with Approve & Apply / Reject buttons that work right from the email.
    • 
          
  • Schedule questions ("send me the schedule for Oct 11–14") → the sender gets an automatic reply with the schedule. Draft shifts are labeled (draft).
    • 
          
  • Plans show who requested the change (name, email, and CC).
    • 
        

      


      

        
Team members & Invite new team members

        

          
Team members — profiles, invites, and status

          
Invite new team members — share the application link, review candidates

        

        
Team members — add people manually or approve them from the candidate queue. Each member sets their own password via an email invite.

        
Invite new team members — people apply through your application link. New applications email you a Review Application link where you can edit their details and Approve or Reject without logging in. Approving creates their team member account and sends them an invite.

        
Set special instructions per member and an optional end date (stops future shifts, keeps history).

      


      

        
Rotations

        

          
Rotations — repeating patterns per team member

        

        
Build repeating patterns (1–84 days) under Settings → Rotations. Assign team members to cycle days and the schedule fills in automatically. Manual shifts always take precedence over rotation-generated ones.

        
You can pause a rotation for vacations and exclude individual rotation instances (e.g. someone swapped out for one day).

      


      

        
Sharable Links

        

          
Sharable Links — schedule, availability, and open-slot links

        

        
No-login links for people outside the team:

        
    
          
  • Schedule link — view-only schedule for temps or non-team staff. Filter by department or shift type.
    • 
          
  • Open shifts broadcast — generate a message listing open shifts for a date range, ready to copy-paste to a group chat.
    • 
          
  • Availability link — staff mark themselves available/unavailable for dates you choose; you see responses per person.
    • 
        

      


      

        
Payroll

        
Pick a date range to see hours by date plus per-person totals. Filter by person, download a PDF, or email the report. Automatic weekly/monthly payroll emails can be scheduled from the payroll tab.

      


      

        
Run report (audit log)

        
The Run report button (top of every tab, bottom of the Schedule page, and on each date popup) shows who changed the schedule — what changed, when, who did it, and whether it was manual or from a rotation. Tracking started October 9, 2026.

      


      


      


      

        
What the employee view looks like

        

          

            
            
Employee: shared schedule link — clean view-only schedule, no login needed

          

          

            
            
Employee: availability link — tap dates green/gray for available/unavailable, no login needed

          

        

      


      

        
Viewing your schedule

        
Your scheduler shares a schedule link with you — no login needed. Open it on your phone or computer to see your upcoming shifts. Only published shifts are shown, so what you see is final.

        
On phones, swipe sideways to move between days, or tap a date in the mini calendar at the top to jump straight to it.

      


      

        
Marking your availability

        
When your scheduler sends an availability link, open it and mark yourself available or unavailable for each date. Your scheduler sees everyone's responses and builds the schedule from them.

        
Submit early — late responses may miss the scheduling window.

      


      

        
Setting up your account

        
If your scheduler adds you as a team member, you'll get an invite email. Click the link to set your own password. Your email is your username.

      


      

        
Requesting schedule changes

        
Need a swap or a day off? Email your scheduler — the system reads schedule emails automatically. For swaps, include who you'd like to swap with and the date. You'll be notified once it's approved.

        
You can also email a question like "Am I scheduled next weekend?" and get an automatic reply with your shifts.

      


      

        
Applying for a job

        
Applied through a company's application link? You'll get an email invite if you're approved. Check your spam folder if you don't see it within a few days.

Help & How-to

        
Everything you can do as a team member, in one place.

      


      

        
My schedule views

        

          

            
            
Week view — your shifts marked YOU, Swap and Ask for cover on your own shifts

          

          

            
            
Month view on desktop — full calendar grid

          

          

            
            
Month view on phones — mini date grid on top, tap a date to jump; teal = your shifts

          

        

        
My schedule shows the whole team's schedule — your shifts and everyone else's.

        
    
          
  • Day — everyone working on one date, with full shift names.
    • 
          
  • Week — seven days at a glance.
    • 
          
  • Month — on phones: a mini date grid on top (tap a date to jump) plus a sliding two-day strip below. Teal dots mark your shifts, gray dots mark others'.
    • 
          
  • Range — pick any date range.
    • 
        

        
Green dates are published (final), gray are draft, plain are empty.

      


      

        
Filters

        

          

            
            
Department, Shift, and View filters above the schedule

          

        

        
Three dropdowns above the schedule:

        
    
          
  • Department — show one department or all.
    • 
          
  • Shift — show one shift type or all.
    • 
          
  • View — My shifts (only yours), Everyone's schedule (the full team), or Available shifts (open shifts you can pick up).
    • 
        

      


      

        
Picking up open shifts

        

          

            
            
Available shifts view — tap Pick up to claim an open shift

          

        

        
Set the View filter to Available shifts to see open shifts across the whole date range. Tap Pick up to claim one.

        
    
          
  • You can only pick up a shift on a date you're not already working.
    • 
        

      


      

        
Swap & ask for cover

        

          

            
            
Your shifts show Swap and Ask for cover buttons

          

        

        
On your own shifts you can:

        
    
          
  • Swap — offer your shift to a teammate in exchange for one of theirs.
    • 
          
  • Ask for cover — ask someone to take your shift. Cover requests need approval.
    • 
        

        
Other people's shifts are read-only — you can't change them.

      


      

        
Shift requests

        

          

            
            
Shift requests — Received and Sent sub-tabs

          

        

        
The Shift requests tab has two sub-tabs:

        
    
          
  • Sent — requests you made (pickups, swaps, cover).
    • 
          
  • Received — requests sent to you. A badge on the tab tells you when one needs your action.
    • 
        

      


      

        
Ask Q-Scheduler

        

          

            
            
Ask Q-Scheduler — plain-language questions, instant answers

          

          

            
            
Email Q-Scheduler — write your request, it opens your email app

          

          

            
            
The Email Q-Scheduler button floats at the bottom-right of every screen

          

        

        
Ask plain-language questions about the schedule, like "Who's working on October 14?" or "Am I scheduled next weekend?"

        
The Email Q-Scheduler button (also floating at the bottom-right of every screen) opens your email app addressed to the AI scheduler — write what you need (a day off, a swap, a question) and the system builds a plan and sends it to your scheduler for approval. You can also attach a file — a PDF, an Excel sheet, a CSV or a Word document (for example a whole schedule) — and the AI reads the attachment as part of your request.
